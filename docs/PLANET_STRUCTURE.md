# Planet Structure & Orchestration

Every celestial object in the portfolio represents a distinct content category and conversion goal.

## 0. The Sun (Arrival)

- **Purpose:** Brand anchor. Sets the premium tone immediately.
- **Visual:** Blindingly warm core, subtle corona.
- **Visitor Goal:** Understand exactly who this person is within 3 seconds.
- **Primary CTA:** "Initiate Journey"
- **Expected Time:** 15 seconds.

## 1. Mercury (About)

- **Purpose:** Establish human connection.
- **Visual:** Rocky, fast-spinning, close to the heat. High contrast lighting.
- **Content:** Story, Mission, Values.
- **Primary CTA:** "View Experience ->"

## 2. Venus (Skills)

- **Purpose:** Hard technical validation.
- **Visual:** Dense, glowing atmosphere. Interactive particles representing nodes of knowledge.
- **Content:** Skills grouped into interactive "Constellations".
- **Primary CTA:** "See Projects in Action ->"

## 3. Earth (The Journey: Experience & Education)

- **Purpose:** Career credibility and trust.
- **Visual:** Familiar, blue, hospitable. Clouds moving slowly.
- **Content:** Vertical timeline of employment history, followed by foundational degrees and certifications.
- **Primary CTA:** "Download Resume"

## 4. Mars (Services & Freelance)

- **Purpose:** B2B Sales pipeline for freelance work.
- **Visual:** Red, dusty, structured.
- **Content:** Service tiers, process explanation, client testimonials (Social Proof), and ideal client profiling.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book Discovery Call" (Cal.com integration)
- **Expected Time:** 2-3 minutes.

## 5. Saturn (Projects)

- **Purpose:** The Crown Jewel. Deep demonstration of engineering and design.
- **Visual:** The most beautiful object. Massive ice rings that cast shadows on the planet.
- **Content:** Filterable case studies. Each project is a detailed deep-dive (Architecture, Stack, Live Demo).
- **Primary CTA:** "View Source Code" / "Visit Live Site"

## 6. Neptune (The Forge: Open Source & Hackathons)

- **Purpose:** Live proof of consistency, passion, and speed of execution.
- **Visual:** Deep, dark, violent blue.
- **Content:** Fetched GitHub GraphQL data showing the contribution graph, top repos, alongside Hackathon wins and awards.
- **Primary CTA:** "Follow on GitHub"

## 7. Pluto (Blog)

- **Purpose:** Show thought leadership (Placeholder).
- **Visual:** Small, distant, icy, mysterious.
- **Content:** "Coming Soon" terminal with topics roadmap.
- **Future Feature:** Headless CMS integration for MDX blog posts.

## 8. Halley's Comet (Contact)

- **Purpose:** The Ultimate Conversion.
- **Visual:** High velocity, glowing tail, erratic orbit.
- **Content:** Contact methods, meeting booking, and availability status.
- **Primary CTA:** "Send Email" or "Book Meeting"
