# Nebula Presets

The following configurations are registered in `nebula-presets.ts`.

### 1. `deep-space`

- **Mood**: Cinematic, cosmic wonder
- **Colors**: Deep Violet background scaling up into warm Solar Orange midgrounds and Cyan teal foreground highlights.
- **Layers**: 3 (Ultra/High)

### 2. `blue-nebula`

- **Mood**: Cold, isolated, technical
- **Colors**: Stark navy and dark blue scaling into pale azure and white filaments.
- **Layers**: 3 (Ultra/High)

### 3. `purple-nebula`

- **Mood**: Fantasy, vibrant, high-energy
- **Colors**: Deep plum evolving into bright magenta and electric purple.
- **Layers**: 3 (Ultra/High)

### 4. `golden-nebula`

- **Mood**: Ancient, majestic, stellar nursery
- **Colors**: Deep browns and burnt umber rising to bright glowing gold and yellow.
- **Layers**: 3 (Ultra/High)

### 5. `minimal-space`

- **Mood**: Performance-first, dark mode
- **Colors**: Nearly black with extremely faint dark blue noise structure.
- **Layers**: 1 (All tiers)
- **Use Case**: Used for strict performance environments or purely focusing on the planetary objects.
