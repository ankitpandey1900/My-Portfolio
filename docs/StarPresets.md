# Starfield Presets

The Starfield Engine supports multiple presets designed to evoke different atmospheric moods.

- **realistic**: Standard mix of warm and cool stars. Good baseline.
- **ultra-dense**: Maximize star count (up to 30,000). Highly dramatic.
- **sparse**: Reduces stars to minimal levels, simulating a less dense region of the galaxy.
- **deep-space**: Pushes stars far away into a massive enclosing shell (outer radius 1000) for wide parallax.
- **minimal**: Strips background noise for extreme performance or minimal aesthetic configurations.
